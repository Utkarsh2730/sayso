FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Sayso work with Wispr Flow?

Click a field in Sayso so the cursor is in it, then hold your Wispr Flow key (fn on a Mac) and talk, just like in any other app. Sayso recognises dictated recaps and shows on the proof page how much was written by voice. Without Wispr Flow, the mic button uses your browser's speech recognition.

What if it gets something wrong?

Say the correction ("her email is actually…") or edit the field directly before you save. Sayso is told never to guess: if you didn't mention an email or amount, that field stays blank instead of being made up.

Can I use it for my own company?

Yes. Click Open app and choose Set up my company: add your company name, your name, what you sell and your currency. You get an empty workspace for your own calls, follow-up emails are signed with your details, and amounts default to your currency. Prefer to look around first? Choose Open the demo.

Do I need an API key?

No. Sayso's built-in engine pulls out names, companies, emails, phone numbers, amounts in lakh, crore or dollars, deal stages, dates and next steps, handles spoken corrections and answers pipeline questions, all without an API key. If you add an Anthropic key, Claude takes over for even better accuracy.

Where is my data stored?

On this website, in your own browser: your company details and calls never reach a server. If you run Sayso yourself, they're kept in a file on that machine, and nothing leaves it unless you turn on HubSpot sync or add an optional Claude key.

Which CRMs does it work with?

It has a built-in CRM and a CSV export for Google Sheets or Excel. When you run Sayso yourself, it can also sync contacts, call notes and follow-up tasks to HubSpot.

How fast is it?

The built-in engine fills the record in a fraction of a second, and the app shows exactly how long it took. Saving is instant; HubSpot syncs afterwards without making you wait.
